# App Store Submission Draft

## App name

StudyBuddy

## Subtitle

Exam study planner and quiz coach

## Description

StudyBuddy helps you stay on track while preparing for certification and school exams. Build a daily study rhythm, review objective-based tasks, drill flashcards, and answer original practice prompts with explanations.

The included starter content is tuned for CompTIA A+ Core 1 220-1201, CompTIA A+ Core 2 220-1202, and CompTIA Security+ SY0-701. StudyBuddy includes an active exam picker, separate local progress for each exam, objective-based plans, randomized flashcards, 10-question short tests, randomized 90-question simulations, and PBQ-style matching and drag-to-order scenarios.

StudyBuddy also includes six stateful, touch-friendly labs across the three exams, including a command terminal, printer workbench, support workstations, firewall configuration, packet testing, and SOC investigation. Guided and Challenge modes make the labs useful for both learning and readiness checks.

The optional AI Tutor learns from attempts and mistakes to recommend targeted review. StudyBuddy stores core progress locally and does not require an account. Protected AI requests use Apple's App Attest service on supported devices.

StudyBuddy is independent study software. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by CompTIA. CompTIA, A+, and Security+ are trademarks of CompTIA, Inc.

## Privacy

StudyBuddy does not require an account and does not collect contact info, location, payment info, advertising data, or tracking data. Because the app uses a hosted AI tutor server, App Store Connect should disclose a random User ID, Product Interaction, Other User Content for AI Tutor messages, and a Device ID for the app-scoped App Attest key identifier. Uses: App Functionality and Product Personalization where applicable. Linked to the user's app profile or device: Yes. Tracking: No.
